# Three hard measures of communication

Copy is communication, and communication has three jobs: get seen, get understood, get acted on.

## 1. Attention

The first step in a conversion is not page logic or price advantage, it is whether the eye stops. Rational assessment happens after attention.

- **Negative words catch more than positive ones.** "Anxious cat? Use a wide flat dish" beats "wide flat dish, comfortable for cats". Words like anxious, noisy, collapsed, leaking, mouldy intercept the buyer before the thinking starts. Titles, main images, and the first A+ screen can all use this.
- **Showing the consequence beats showing the product.** The bacteria you cannot see, the morning breath, the sole that splits after three months.
- Put the difference in the first screen. Buyers spend three seconds asking "how is this different", and if there is no answer, they leave.

Creating tension is not the same as creating false fear. Do not use prohibited medical or safety scare tactics.

## 2. Effort: understood without thinking

"A lingering fragrance that surrounds you for three days" needs translating once. "You will want a second one" needs none. Every layer of translation loses readers. **The first rule of copy is not writing well, it is being understood instantly.**

Four ways to do it:

1. **Plain speech** - write it the way you would recommend it to a friend. "This power bank is ridiculous, a week of travel without charging it."
2. **A specific scene** - not "your feet do not hurt when you walk a lot", but "ten thousand steps and the soles of your feet are still soft".
3. **Physical nouns** - nouns carry pictures. "A refined lifestyle experience" is empty; "the cup, the road, the tree" is not.
4. **Borrow the prototype in memory** - a sentence the buyer already has, which you only need to activate.

## 3. Effect: believed, wanted, remembered

| Weak | Strong | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opinion | Fact | "Carefully selected quality" is translated automatically as bragging; "15 selection steps" has no counter-argument |
| Adjective | Number | "Charges fast" says nothing; "80% in 30 minutes" sounds measured |
| Promise | Evidence | Nobody believes "good waterproofing"; a video of 72 hours underwater is hard to disbelieve |

Different positions pursue different effects: the main image wants memory, the detail page wants the sale, brand content wants goodwill. Do not use one voice for everything.

## The detail page is persuasion, not explanation

While reading a listing the buyer keeps asking: is it really good, will I regret it, how does it compare with the other one, should I buy now or look again. Every block of copy has one job: move that judgement forward by a small step.

- **Attention** - catch the eye with the difference; show the biggest one on the first screen.
- **Interest** - pain point plus claim, so it feels like it might actually help.
- **Desire** - create ownership. Apple angles the laptop screen to 76 degrees so you reach out and touch it.
- **Trust** - answer the unspoken "why should I believe you": repeat purchase rate, awards, measured data, real reviews.
- **Action** - a deadline or a limited quantity is not a trick, it is the last push for someone hesitating.

Do not apply the order mechanically. The point is to remember that the buyer is not reading, they are deciding.

## Spreading a claim across scenes

A claim with no connection to daily life loses its meaning. Take one claim and split it into scenes.

"Your feet do not hurt when you walk" becomes:

- two hours of dancing and your feet are still soft
- a whole day at a theme park with your grandchildren
- walking the dog without wanting to turn back halfway
- a trip to the shops without changing shoes
- no toes pressed against the front on the way downhill

Do the same with pain points. **Claim plus pain point is the sentence that closes the sale**; the second half looks like an aside and is actually the final nudge:

- Slimming, without squeezing your stomach out over the waistband
- Good sound insulation, so you keep sleeping while the neighbours drag chairs above you
- Large capacity, and your shoulder still does not ache after carrying it all day
